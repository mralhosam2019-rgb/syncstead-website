End-of-service calculator

Estimate an employee's end-of-service award under the Saudi Labour Law (Articles 84 and 85).

How it is calculated

  • Half a month's wage for each of the first five years of service, and a full month's wage for each year after that, with part years counted proportionally (Article 84).
  • On resignation: nothing under two years of service, one third of the award from two to five years, two thirds from five to ten years, and the full award after ten years (Article 85).

This is an estimate for guidance only and is not legal advice. Special cases (for example force majeure, or a female employee resigning within six months of marriage or three months of giving birth) can change the result. Syncstead calculates end of service automatically for every employee.
