Saudi Labour Law 2026: the key rules for employers

5 November 2026 · 6 min read

The rules HR teams apply every month, with the article number and what changed in the amendments that took effect in February 2025.

Working time

RuleWhat the law saysArticle
Normal hoursUp to 8 hours a day or 48 a week98
Ramadan (Muslim employees)Up to 6 hours a day or 36 a week98
Weekly restFriday, paid, at least 24 hours in a row; can be moved for some workers after notifying the Labour Office104
OvertimeHourly wage plus 50% of the basic wage; holidays and Eids count as overtime. Since 2025 the parties can agree on paid time off instead.107

Leave

LeaveEntitlementArticle
Annual21 days a year; 30 days after 5 consecutive years. Unused leave is paid when employment ends.109, 111
SickWithin one year, continuous or not: 30 days full pay, then 60 days at 75%, then 30 days unpaid117
Maternity12 weeks at full pay (10 weeks before 2025); 6 weeks must be taken after the birth151
Paternity3 days at full pay, taken within 7 days of the birth113
Marriage5 days at full pay113
Bereavement5 days for a spouse, ascendant or descendant (for example a parent, grandparent, child or grandchild); 3 days for a brother or sister (new in 2025)113
Hajj10 to 15 days including the Eid al-Adha holiday, once, after 2 consecutive years of service114
ExamsPaid for exam days in a year the student is not repeating; request at least 15 days ahead115

Contracts and ending employment

RuleWhat the law saysArticle
ProbationMust be stated in the contract and cannot exceed 180 days in total (changed in 2025)53
Notice (open-ended contracts, monthly paid)At least 30 days from the employee and at least 60 days from the employer (changed in 2025)75
ResignationFor fixed-term contracts: treated as accepted if the employer does not reply within 30 days; the employer can postpone acceptance by up to 60 days if business needs require it, with written reasons; the employee can withdraw within 7 days if not yet accepted (new in 2025)79 bis
End-of-service awardHalf a month's wage for each of the first 5 years, a month's wage for each year after; reduced on resignation84, 85
