Privacy notice

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

Syncstead Company (a simplified joint stock company, CR 7055468719), Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, operates this website. Contact: info@syncstead.com.sa.

What we collect

  • Details you send us through the demo request form, email or WhatsApp: your name, work email, company, number of employees and, if you add it, your mobile number. The form also records which page you came from and, if present, the campaign link you used.
  • Booking details you enter in our Google Calendar booking page.

Demo requests are saved in our company Google Workspace (Google Sheets) and emailed to our team, so we can reply. Nothing is stored on the website itself.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, arrange a meeting and send you a proposal you asked for. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; these services may log your IP address as part of delivering the page. Demo requests are stored and emailed through Google Workspace, and bookings are handled by Google Calendar. We measure visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify you.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to follow up, and delete them on request or when no longer needed.

Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent. Email info@syncstead.com.sa and we will respond promptly.

Client data in the Syncstead platform

Employee data that clients load into the Syncstead platform is covered by a separate data processing agreement signed before any real data is used.
