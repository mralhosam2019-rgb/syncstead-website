Final settlement calculator

Estimate everything due to an employee when they leave: the end-of-service award, pay for unused annual leave and the salary for days worked in the last month, less any deductions.

How it is calculated

  • End-of-service award: half a month's wage for each of the first five years and a full month's wage for each year after (Article 84), reduced on resignation (Article 85).
  • Unused annual leave: 21 days a year, 30 after five years (Article 109), paid at the monthly wage divided by 30 (Article 111).
  • Last month's salary: the monthly wage divided by 30, times the days worked and not yet paid.

This is an estimate for guidance only and is not legal advice. Notice-period pay, compensation for unlawful termination and special cases are not included. Syncstead calculates this automatically for every employee.
