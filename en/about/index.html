About Syncstead

A Saudi company, built by HR practitioners.

Our mission: give every Saudi organization full control of its workforce, cost and compliance, in one system its people actually enjoy using.

A message from our CEO

Your people decisions deserve the same rigour as your financial ones.

“

Saudi companies are changing how they manage people. Saudization targets, evolving labour regulations and leadership teams that expect every decision to come with its cost.

Yet many HR teams still run payroll on spreadsheets, or wait months while global systems are customized for Saudi rules. I founded Syncstead to close that gap.

After years advising Saudi organizations on HR and governance, I built the platform I wanted my clients to have: Saudi regulation at its core, Arabic and English as equals, and governance designed in from the first screen.

Our commitment is simple: payroll you can trust to the riyal, decisions with their cost in front of you, and a partner who understands how your organization works.

Al Hosam Al Ghamdi
Founder & CEO
